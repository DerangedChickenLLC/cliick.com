Start a Cliick

It starts empty. You fill it.

Cliick opens on a room with nobody in it but you. Post on day one if you like — it goes to your Peeps, where you are the only member, so you are the only one who sees it.

It is yours before it is anyone else’s.

Every post is addressed.

When you post, you choose which Cliick it goes to, and the people in that Cliick are the ones who see it. That is what a Cliick is for — you decide who is in the room.

Add someone next month and they see what came before. Cliick keeps your history, so anything you put in early is still there when your people arrive.

Organize it however suits you.

You connect with people in person — they scan your code, or you send them a link. From there you decide where everyone belongs, and you can change your mind whenever you like.

A few close friends

Use Peeps and stop there. Everyone you add sees what you post. Nothing to set up, nothing to name.

One group in mind

Make a Cliick for it. The book club, the cousins, the people you travel with — one place, one purpose.

Lots of separate circles

Make several, and send each post to the one it belongs in. Your grandmother and your climbing friends never have to meet.

Get the app, then make it yours.

Cliick is a free app for iPhone and Android. Your first post can happen before anyone else is there.
