About

Meet Cliick

It feels great to connect again.

Social media promised to bring us closer. And for a while — it did. Somewhere between the ads, the algorithms, and the uninvited strangers in your feed, real connection got lost in the sauce.

Cliick is your reset.

Share with the people who matter.

No public feed. No algorithm deciding what you see. No strangers. Just the people you know, sharing real moments in the order they happen — and then getting on with their lives.

Everything in its right place

Cliicks are private groups you create and invite people to — family, friends, coworkers, neighbors, artists. Each group only sees what you intend. So your grandmother doesn't see your Burning Man photos, and your friends don't see hers.

The Cliick Difference

Funded by members. Not advertisers.

Most platforms make money by selling your attention and your data. Cliick is funded by its members — which means no ads, no data mining, and no pressure to do things we're not proud of. You're not just paying for an app. You're paying for one that respects your privacy.

Ready to jump in?

Cliick is free to start and joining couldn't be easier.

  1. Step 1 — Download Cliick

    Available on iOS and Android.

  2. Step 2 — Sign in

    Apple or Google — we don't ask for your phone number, email, or contact list.

  3. Step 3 — Make it yours

    Choose a name, add a profile photo, and select a membership level. Cliick is free to start.

  4. Step 4 — Invite your Peeps

    Your best friends, your family, your college crew, your neighbors, your team. Every person you invite shapes your network.

  5. Step 5 — Share with confidence

    Photos, videos, messages — posted directly to the people or groups you choose, in the order they were shared. No algorithms, no surprises.

Who's behind Cliick?

A small team that got tired of the wasteland — and the companies profiting from your desire to stay connected. We're not trying to build the next big social platform. We're trying to build something better, more private, and more human — for people who want to share their life without having it sold out from under them.
