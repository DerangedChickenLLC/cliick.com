Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a private circle?

To create a private circle, tap the "+" button on your main screen and select "Create Circle." You can then invite friends by sharing your unique circle code.

Can I change my display name?

Yes! Go to Settings > Profile and tap on your current name to change it. Your new name will be visible to all your circle members.

Is my data private and secure?

Absolutely. We use end-to-end encryption for all your messages and don't share your data with third parties. Read our Privacy Policy for more details.

How do I delete my account?

You can delete your account by going to Settings > Account > Delete Account. Please note that this action is permanent and cannot be undone.
