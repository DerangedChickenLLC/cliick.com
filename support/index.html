Frequently Asked Questions

What is Cliick?

Cliick is a private, ad-free social network focused on authentic connections with people you actually know.

Who can see my posts?

Your posts are only visible to people inside the Cliicks (groups) you’ve shared them with. There are no public feeds.

Is my data sold or used for ads?

No. Cliick is built to be private and ad-free. We don’t sell your data or track you across the web.

How do I report an issue?

You can use the contact form below or email us directly at support@cliick.app.
