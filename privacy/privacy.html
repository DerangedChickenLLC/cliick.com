Welcome

Welcome to Cliick!

At Cliick, your privacy is our top priority. We built Cliick to be a safe and private space for friends, family, and trusted groups — without ads, without hidden tracking, and without selling your data. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect (and what we don’t), how we use it, how we protect it, and the rights you have regarding your data. By using Cliick, you agree to the practices described in this Policy. If we make changes, we’ll update this page and include a new effective date.

1. Age & Eligibility

Cliick is intended for users who are at least 13 years old. If you are under 18, you may only use the app with the consent of a parent or guardian. Parents and guardians may also request access to or deletion of their child’s information by contacting us. We comply with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided personal data, we will delete it promptly.

2. What We Collect (and What We Don’t)

We believe the best way to protect your privacy is to collect as little as possible. When you create or use a Cliick account, we collect a randomly generated app-specific identifier from Apple or Google login. If you choose to provide a display name or profile image, we store that as well. We also store the messages, photos, and media you share with others inside Cliick, along with basic technical details such as IP address and device type to ensure security and functionality.

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, birthdays, or mailing addresses. We do not track your location, access your contact list, or collect device advertising identifiers. We do not monitor your browsing history, usage patterns, or build behavioral profiles. We also do not collect sensitive data such as information about your health, race, political views, or biometrics. Most importantly, Cliick does not share your information with advertisers, does not build advertising-based user profiles, and will never monetize your content.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use the limited data we collect only to provide and maintain Cliick. Your user ID allows us to recognize your account and keep you logged in. Your content is stored so that you and others you share with can access it inside Cliick. Technical information like IP address and device type helps us protect against abuse, troubleshoot problems, and improve performance. We do not analyze your content, run algorithms on it, or sell your information. You are not the product.

4. Third-Party Services

Cliick relies on a small number of trusted third-party providers. Apple supports authentication and subscription payments. Google provides authentication services. We also use secure cloud infrastructure providers for storage and backups. These partners are contractually required to protect your data and may not use it for any other purpose. A current list of our infrastructure providers is available upon request. For details about how Apple and Google handle data, please see their respective privacy policies.

5. Data Storage & Security

Your data is encrypted both when it is transmitted and while it is stored. We keep only the minimum amount of data necessary to operate the app. No system is completely secure, but we follow industry best practices to safeguard your information, including encryption, strict access controls, and regular system monitoring.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

You control your data in Cliick. You may delete your account or any content you own at any time, and once deleted, it is permanently removed from our servers. Content that you share in other users’ Cliicks may remain visible in that group even after you delete your account, so if you want that content removed, you should delete it before leaving the group.

For security and reliability, temporary backups may exist briefly after deletion but are purged in line with our data destruction policy. Backups are retained no longer than 90 days.

7. International Data Transfers

Cliick’s servers are located in the United States. If you use the app from outside the U.S., your data may be transferred to and processed here. Where required by law, we use safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses to ensure compliance with international data protection standards like the GDPR.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, privacy laws such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) in Europe or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) in the United States may give you rights over your personal data. These rights include the ability to access your data, correct or amend it, delete your account and associated data, restrict or object to processing, and request a copy of your data in a portable format. To exercise these rights, you may contact us at privacy@cliick.com.

9. Cookies, Tracking & Analytics

Cliick does not use cookies, tracking pixels, fingerprinting, or analytics tools. We do not monitor your actions inside or outside of the app. If we introduce browser-based features in the future that require cookies, we will update this Privacy Policy to clearly explain their use.

10. Breach Notification Protocol

In the event of a data breach, we will notify affected users promptly as required by law. Because we do not collect email addresses, all notifications will be delivered inside the app. We maintain internal protocols for immediate investigation, containment, and resolution of any breach.

11. Legal Basis for Processing

Where GDPR or similar laws apply, we process data under the following legal bases: your consent (when you create and use an account), performance of a contract (to deliver Cliick’s services), and legitimate interests (such as ensuring security, preventing abuse, and maintaining performance).

12. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, new legal requirements, or technical updates. If the changes are material, we will communicate them in-app and post the new effective date at the top of this page.

13. Governing Law

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of the State of New Jersey, United States, without regard to conflict-of-law principles.

14. Oversight & Enforcement

If you believe Cliick is not complying with this Privacy Policy, you may contact us directly or file a complaint with your local data protection authority. We regularly review our privacy practices to ensure compliance with legal requirements and alignment with our commitment to user trust.

15. Contact Us
