Questions? Answers.

Getting started

What is Cliick?

Cliick is a private, member-funded social network. There's no public feed, no algorithm, and no strangers — just the people and groups you actually choose, seeing what you actually share, in the order you shared it.

How do I create an account?

Download Cliick from the App Store or Google Play, then sign in with your existing Apple or Google account. We don't ask for your phone number, email, or contact list to get started.

What's a "Cliick"?

A Cliick is a private group you create and invite people to — your family, your close friends, a team, a neighborhood, a shared hobby. Each Cliick only sees what you choose to share with it, and nothing crosses over between groups.

How do invites work?

Every Cliick is invite-only. As the creator, you send a link to the people you want in — only they can join, and you can remove anyone at any time.

Membership & pricing

What's the difference between Guest and Premium?

Both plans include full access to every feature — the difference is how many people can join a single Cliick. Guest is free forever and supports up to 6 members per Cliick (you and 5 others). Premium is $6/month and raises that to 250 members per Cliick (you and 249 others), for larger families, teams, or communities.

Is there a free plan?

Yes — Guest is free forever, with no credit card required to sign up.

Can I switch between monthly and annual billing?

Annual billing is $72/year. Monthly pricing is coming soon — until then, annual is the only paid option available.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. You can cancel your Premium membership at any time, and you'll keep access through the end of your current billing period. Your account simply reverts to the free Guest plan afterward — nothing is deleted.

Privacy & safety

Does Cliick sell my data?

No. Cliick will never sell your data or personal info, ever. We're funded entirely by our members — not by advertisers, trackers, or data mining — so there's no incentive to do anything with your information except keep your account running.

Who can see what I post?

Only the members of the specific Cliick you post to. There's no public feed and no cross-posting between groups — your grandmother's Cliick and your friend group's Cliick never see each other's content.

Are there ads on Cliick?

None. Cliick is ad-free, always — that's the whole point of being member-funded instead of ad-funded.

Is there an algorithm deciding what I see?

No. Your feed shows exactly what the people in your Cliicks share, in the order they shared it — nothing ranked, boosted, or hidden by an algorithm.

Using Cliick

Can I be in more than one Cliick?

Yes — join or create as many Cliicks as you like. Your family Cliick, your friend group, your team at work — each stays completely separate from the others.

What can I share on Cliick?

Photos, videos, and messages, posted directly to the people or groups you choose. Everything shows up in the order it was shared — no surprises.

What platforms is Cliick available on?

Cliick is available now on iOS and Android.
