Quick Start Amazingness

Don't worry

This is gonna be easy

Cliick is free to start. Follow these five simple steps and you'll be inviting people in no time.

Step 1.

Download Cliick

Available on iOS and Android.

Step 2.

Sign in

Apple or Google — we don't ask for your phone number, email, or contact list.

Step 3.

Make it yours

Choose a name, add a profile photo, and select a membership level. Cliick is free to start.

Step 4.

Invite your Peeps

Your best friends, your family, your college crew, your neighbors, your team. Every person you invite shapes your network.

Step 5.

Share with confidence

Photos, videos, messages — posted directly to the people or groups you choose, in the order they were shared. No algorithms, no surprises.

Cliick comes to life the more people you add. Once you have a few, try creating a Cliick and adding them to it.

To create a Cliick, tap the chicken at the top of your feed, you'll figure it out from there.
