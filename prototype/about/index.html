Why we built Cliick

A private space for the people who actually matter

Cliick started with a simple idea: sharing your life shouldn't mean performing for an audience of strangers. No public feed, no ad targeting, no algorithm deciding what you see — just your people, your groups, and your rules.

Built around your real circles

Family, close friends, teammates, the group chat that never sleeps — a Cliick is whatever circle you say it is. Every member joins by invite only, so the only faces in your feed are ones you actually recognize.

Everything, in one place

Cliicks, direct messages, and shared albums live together in one calm dashboard — no ads wedged between posts, no suggested content, no notifications engineered to pull you back in.

Getting started

Jump in, in minutes

  1. 1. Create your account

    Sign up with just your name and number — no email required.

  2. 2. Start a Cliick

    Create a private space for a family, a friend group, or a team.

  3. 3. Invite your people

    Send an invite link. Only the people you choose can join.

  4. 4. Post like nobody's watching

    Share freely — it never leaves the Cliicks you're actually in.
