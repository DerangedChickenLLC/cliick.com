Community & Safety Guidelines

How we keep Cliick respectful, private, and secure for everyone connected.

Overview

  • Community Guidelines
  • Law Enforcement
  • Report

Cliick is a private, ad‑free space made for authentic moments with the people you actually know. To keep that space safe, respectful, and meaningful, these guidelines outline the expectations for all users. Violations may result in content removal, feature restrictions, or account suspension.

Community Guidelines

We want Cliick to feel like a trusted circle. Content and behavior inside your Cliicks should reflect respect for the people connected to you. The following are not permitted anywhere on Cliick:

  • Harassment, bullying, threats, or targeted intimidation.
  • Hate speech or discriminatory content based on race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or any protected characteristic.
  • Sexually explicit content involving minors or content that sexualizes minors. We have zero tolerance.
  • Non‑consensual sharing of private images or personal information ("doxxing").
  • Spam, scams, deceptive practices, or attempts to artificially manipulate engagement.
  • Malware distribution, technical exploitation, or attempts to access other users’ data.

Allowed content should be legal, consensual, and appropriate for the audiences you intentionally connect with. Use discretion when sharing graphic or sensitive material: if it could surprise or upset someone, reconsider before posting.

Protect privacy: Only share photos, videos, or personal details that you have the right to share. Respect the boundaries expressed by your connections.

Law Enforcement

Cliick responds to valid legal requests consistent with applicable laws and our commitment to user privacy. We evaluate all requests to ensure they are properly scoped and lawful. We may reject or narrow requests that are overly broad or lack required legal process.

Data we store is intentionally limited. When legally compelled, we may provide:

  • Basic account identifiers (such as email or username).
  • Login or access timestamps, if retained.
  • Specific content shared, if a lawful order requires it and it still exists on our systems.

We do not provide: speculative access, backdoors, or real‑time monitoring tools. Emergency disclosure requests (imminent risk to life or safety) are prioritized and reviewed carefully. Law enforcement seeking data should contact us through official channels at legal@cliick.com.

Report

If you encounter content or behavior that violates these guidelines, please report it. Reports help us act quickly and keep the platform trustworthy.

When reporting, include as much detail as possible (which Cliick, the content type, approximate time, screenshots if safe). We triage reports based on severity: child safety, threats of violence, and exploitation are addressed first.

  • Email urgent concerns to safety@cliick.com.
  • For general issues or mistakes, use in‑app reporting tools (coming soon).
  • False or abusive reporting may itself violate our guidelines.

Appeals: If your content was removed or your account restricted and you believe it was in error, contact appeals@cliick.com. We review appeals to ensure fairness and refine our processes.

Thank you for helping make Cliick a safe, intentional space. These guidelines may evolve as we grow—check back for updates.
